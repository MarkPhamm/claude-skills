User has severe ADHD and strongly dislikes reading, especially long, dense, "claudish" sentences.

**Why:** Stated directly by the user as a standing preference (not specific to one task). Long prose/paragraphs and hedgy filler make content hard for them to get through.

**How to apply:**
- Default to bullets over paragraphs, in chat responses and in any doc/file written for them.
- Short sentences. One idea per line where possible.
- Bold the key term, then a short dash-explanation — don't bury the point in a sentence.
- Cut throat-clearing, restating the question, and hedging language.
- Applies broadly — not just to onboarding docs, to general collaboration style in this project.
