The user has severe ADHD. They want everything in TL;DR form, conversational and with few words. That is how they take notes, and they want replies written the same way (said 2026-10-27).

How they learn, seen in the Salesforce questions session (2026-10-26):
- Like a data modeller: first "what's X" for each thing, then "how does X link to Y". They build the ERD in their head before writing SQL.
- Bottom up: smallest grain first (offer, reservation, entitlement), then member, account, host, lookups.
- They keep the outline they chose. Reorder inside it; don't regroup it.
- Short, raw, plain questions. Notes are prompts for talking to a person, not polished docs.
- They learn from people and the data more than from docs. Jargon gets split into its pieces in a few plain words.

**How to apply:** Write chat replies, notes and question lists like a quick chat with a teammate: TL;DR first, short lines, plain words, no ceremony. Related: [[be-concise]], [[explain-jargon-and-acronyms]], [[notes-structure-and-grain-order]].
